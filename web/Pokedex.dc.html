Nothing waiting. Anything you mark as "Caught, not yet in HOME" in the Home PC will appear here.
1Gen IV game to Gen V game. In Black, White, Black 2 or White 2, go to the Poké Transfer Lab on Route 15. You need two DS systems: one with the Gen IV game and one with the Gen V game. It sends six at a time through a short mini-game. Pokémon that know an HM move can't be sent, so delete those moves first. Skip this step if you caught it in a Gen V game.
2Gen V game to Pokémon Bank. Put the Gen V cartridge in a 3DS that already has Poké Transporter and Pokémon Bank installed — neither can be downloaded any more. Transporter takes everything in Box 1 of the game, so put exactly what you want to send there. HM moves block it again.
3Pokémon Bank to HOME. In HOME on the Switch, choose to move Pokémon from Pokémon Bank. This is one way: nothing goes back to Bank. The ball, OT and ribbons stay with each Pokémon throughout.
·From a Game Boy Advance game, add a step before 1: use Pal Park in a Gen IV game, which needs an original DS or DS Lite with the GBA cartridge in the second slot.
Only catchable in a Luxury Ball on a DS game: Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold, SoulSilver, Black, White, Black 2 or White 2. Later games either don't have them in the wild or only give them as gifts.
